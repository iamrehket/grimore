**Load-bearing decision**: a choice that is expensive to reverse - reversing it
later would cost more than re-doing the change that introduced it - or that
other parts of a change silently assume. The test is the cost of reversal, not
how interesting the choice felt at the time.

_Avoid_: key decision, major decision.
