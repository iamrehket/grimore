# A rejected component status

Components have three statuses - draft, current, superseded - and no fourth
for options considered and turned down. A rejected alternative is recorded
inside the body of the component that records the decision actually taken,
where a reader meets it alongside the reasoning, rather than as a separate
component that renders nowhere and accumulates in the store. Excluded because
a status that never appears in any consumer output is bookkeeping, not
documentation.
